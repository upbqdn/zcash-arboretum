Source: the CTAN garamond-math package, as shipped in the Tectonic bundle.

Modification: Garamond-Math draws each mathematical italic Greek capital that
looks like a Latin one (Alpha, Beta, Epsilon, Zeta, Eta, Iota, Kappa, Mu, Nu,
Omicron, Rho, Tau, Chi; U+1D6E2-U+1D6FA) with the Latin italic glyph, and PDF
text extraction then reports italic Latin capitals as Greek. Those 13 cmap
entries are removed; glyphs and names are unchanged.
