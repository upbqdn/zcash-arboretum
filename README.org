#+AUTHOR: m@rek.onl
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The Zcash Arboretum is formal, full-depth documentation of the
[[https://z.cash/][Zcash]] protocol, focused on the deployed Orchard and Ironwood
core and the protocols being built on top of it. Each volume states its
dependencies and is checked against the protocol specification, applicable
ZIPs, and deployed implementations.

These materials are *non-normative*: they explain and motivate the
constructions. The [[https://zips.z.cash/protocol/protocol.pdf][Zcash protocol
specification]], applicable [[https://zips.z.cash/][ZIPs]], and consensus rules
remain authoritative. Where this text disagrees with them, this text is wrong.

* NU7 specification snapshot

The NU7 review is pinned to 23 September 2026. The merged but still Draft
[[https://github.com/zcash/zips/blob/afa086bd976e316612a5c06fb139429958d07d84/zips/zip-0259.md][ZIP 259]]
specifies 25-second blocks, halving-preserving Network Sustainability
Mechanism issuance, and rejection of version 4 transactions; versions 5 and 6
retain their formats. Activation and reissuance heights remain unassigned.
The guides explain this target directly, distinguishing open amendments
such as the Ironwood action limits from merged rules. Unresolved source
conflicts are recorded in [[file:research/nu7-2026-09-23.md][the NU7 review]].

* The volumes

Layered; read each atop the ones it names.

** Foundations
1. *Math Guide* (=math-guide.tex=) — the algebraic and number-theoretic
   groundwork: groups, finite fields, elliptic curves, and the relevant
   hardness assumptions.
2. *Crypto Guide* (=crypto-guide.tex=) — the provable-security model, the
   DLP/CDH/DDH hierarchy, hashes, commitments, PRFs and ciphers, key agreement,
   signatures, zero-knowledge and SNARKs, and Merkle trees, developed only as
   far as the upper volumes require.
3. *Halo 2 Guide* (=halo2-guide.tex=) — the Orchard proof system: PLONKish
   arithmetisation and the inner-product commitment on a worked example over
   a 97-element field, the deployed protocol and its knowledge soundness, and
   recursion and accumulation, which Orchard does not deploy.

** Deployed protocol
4. *Consensus Guide* (=consensus-guide.tex=) — Nakamoto consensus: the
   double-spend race in the first-seen model (after Rosenfeld), the
   arithmetic of confirmations, and the deployed most-work rule, including
   implementation divergences and local safety rails; the NU7 timing,
   shielded-work limits, halving schedule, and reserve accounting.
5. *Ironwood Guide* (=ironwood-guide.tex=) — the Orchard protocol for a
   shielded payment in its Ironwood pool: keys and addresses, notes and their
   commitments, the commitment tree, nullifiers, spend authorisation, value
   commitments and the binding signature, the Action statement and its proof,
   note encryption, and the transaction rules, closing with theorems of value
   conservation, double-spend resistance, spend authority, and privacy.
6. *Wallet Guide* (=wallet-guide.tex=) — the protocols a wallet runs: key and
   address derivation from one seed, note discovery through an untrusted
   light-client server and what that server learns, and the construction,
   authorisation, and submission of payments.

** Frontier
7. *FlyClient Guide* (=flyclient-guide.tex=) — Merkle mountain ranges, the
   FlyClient sampling protocol and its security theorem, the ZIP 221
   chain-history commitment, and the boundary between protocol and commitment.
8. *ZSA Guide* (=zsa-guide.tex=) — Zcash Shielded Assets: asset identity,
    issuance, transfer, burn, the global issuance state, and their security.
9. *FROST Guide* (=frost-guide.tex=) — threshold RedPallas: FROST as its
    paper constructs it and RFC 9591 standardises it, the rerandomized form,
    and the surfaces that use it.

Compiled PDFs are committed alongside the sources. [[file:arboretum-complete.pdf][The Complete Arboretum]]
combines all Arboretum volumes into one document; the landing page publishes it
in Web and PDF formats.

* Method

Protocol-level claims are checked against the protocol specification and the
deployed sources (librustzcash, orchard, zebra, and the relevant wallet code).
Review covers numbers, concepts, source currency, and cross-references. Shared
notation and layering rules live in
[[file:CONVENTIONS.org][CONVENTIONS.org]].

* Scope

The material includes only what lies on the critical path to understanding the
documented protocols; off-path generality is deliberately pruned. Everything
that does lie on that path is explained in full. Deprecation-bound layers
(Sprout, Sapling, the transparent layer, the legacy network stack) are
documented only where the surviving protocols depend on them.

* Building

Each volume compiles independently with
[[https://tectonic-typesetting.github.io/][Tectonic]]:

#+begin_src sh
tectonic -Z shell-escape <volume>.tex
#+end_src

The =-Z shell-escape= flag is required. To run the full PDF gate, including
parked research, the talk, and the generated complete edition:

#+begin_src sh
python3 -B site/tools/check_build.py
#+end_src

The gate writes PDFs and logs under =build/pdf/= and fails on overfull boxes,
unresolved references, duplicate labels or destinations, missing glyphs, or
compilation errors. Add =--update-pdfs= to refresh the committed PDFs after
every document passes. CI runs this gate, the site-tooling regression check,
and the =research/check_*.py= numerical checks on pushes and pull
requests.

The website workflow also checks every generated local link and anchor with
=python3 -B site/tools/check_site.py _site= after building its search index.
External sources and mathematical claims still need human review; a clean
build is not a proof of correctness.
