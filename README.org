#+AUTHOR: m@rek.onl

The Zcash Arboretum is formal, full-depth documentation of the
[[https://z.cash/][Zcash]] protocol, focused on the deployed Orchard and Ironwood
core and the protocols being built on top of it. Each volume states its
dependencies and is checked against the protocol specification, applicable
ZIPs, and deployed implementations.

These materials are *non-normative*: they explain and motivate the
constructions. The [[https://zips.z.cash/protocol/protocol.pdf][Zcash protocol
specification]], applicable [[https://zips.z.cash/][ZIPs]], and consensus rules
remain authoritative. Where this text disagrees with them, this text is wrong.

* The volumes

Layered; read each atop the ones it names.

** Foundations
1. *Math Guide* (=math-guide.tex=) — the algebraic and number-theoretic
   groundwork: groups, finite fields, elliptic curves, and the relevant
   hardness assumptions.
2. *Crypto Guide* (=crypto-guide.tex=) — commitments, hashes, signatures,
   zero-knowledge, and the DLP/CDH/DDH hierarchy, developed only as far as the
   upper volumes require.
3. *Halo 2 Guide* (=halo2-guide.tex=) — PLONKish arithmetisation, the
   permutation, lookup, and vanishing arguments, the inner-product polynomial
   commitment, the Halo accumulation trick, and recursive proof composition,
   closing with one worked statement carried end to end, every number real.

** Deployed protocol
4. *Consensus Guide* (=consensus-guide.tex=) — Nakamoto consensus: the
   double-spend race in the first-seen model (after Rosenfeld), the
   arithmetic of confirmations, and the deployed most-work rule, including
   implementation divergences and local safety rails.
5. *Ironwood Guide* (=ironwood-guide.tex=) — notes, commitments, nullifiers,
   the commitment tree and authentication paths, the key hierarchy, note
   encryption, value balance, the Action statement and circuit, the two-pool
   split (legacy Orchard, current Ironwood), and the deployed stack's
   post-quantum security boundary.
6. *Wallet Guide* (=wallet-guide.tex=) — the wallet layer: ZIP-32 hierarchical
   key derivation, unified addresses (ZIP-316), fees (ZIP-317), and the
   transaction-construction pipeline as deployed in librustzcash.
7. *Sync Guide* (=sync-guide.tex=) — light-client synchronisation: ZIP-307
   compact blocks, the lightwalletd, Zaino, and Zebra service surfaces, the
   scanning pipeline, and — stated plainly — what the server learns.

** Frontier
8. *FlyClient Guide* (=flyclient-guide.tex=) — succinct chain verification:
   the FlyClient sampling protocol and its security analysis, the deployed
   ZIP-221 chain-history commitment, and the unbuilt bridge between them.
9. *ZSA Guide* (=zsa-guide.tex=) — Zcash Shielded Assets: issuance,
    transfer, and the counterfeiting boundary.
10. *Crosslink Guide* (=crosslink-guide.tex=) — trailing finality: the
    ebb-and-flow model, the Crosslink construction, stake and delegation,
    and the wallet-visible contract two ledgers create.
11. *FROST Guide* (=frost-guide.tex=) — threshold RedPallas: FROST as its
    paper constructs it and RFC 9591 standardises it, the rerandomized form,
    and the surfaces that use it.

Compiled PDFs are committed alongside the sources. [[file:arboretum-complete.pdf][The Complete Arboretum]]
combines all Arboretum volumes into one document; the landing page publishes it
in Web and PDF formats.

* Method

Protocol-level claims are checked against the protocol specification and the
deployed sources (librustzcash, orchard, zebra, and the relevant wallet code).
Review covers numbers, concepts, source currency, and cross-references. Shared
notation and layering rules live in
[[file:CONVENTIONS.org][CONVENTIONS.org]].

* Scope

The material includes only what lies on the critical path to understanding the
documented protocols; off-path generality is deliberately pruned. Everything
that does lie on that path is explained in full. Deprecation-bound layers
(Sprout, Sapling, the transparent layer, the legacy network stack) are
documented only where the surviving protocols depend on them.

* Building

Each volume compiles independently with
[[https://tectonic-typesetting.github.io/][Tectonic]]:

#+begin_src sh
tectonic -Z shell-escape <volume>.tex
#+end_src

The =-Z shell-escape= flag is required. CI builds every =*-guide.tex= document
on push, including the parked research.
